# 🏥 Smart Health — AI-Driven Health Center & Supply Chain Management

**Smart Health** is a web-based healthcare management dashboard designed to help health centers monitor patients, manage medicine inventory, track bed availability, and improve supply chain visibility.

## ✨ Features

- 📊 **Dashboard:** Overview of patients, visits, bed availability, and medicine stock.
- 🧑‍⚕️ **Patient Management:** View patient queue and basic patient records.
- 💊 **Medicine Inventory:** Monitor medicine stock and identify low-stock items.
- 🤖 **AI Forecasting:** Explore estimated demand and healthcare resource forecasts.
- 🛏️ **Bed Management:** Track bed availability.
- 👩‍⚕️ **Doctors & Staff:** View healthcare staff information.
- 🚚 **Supply Chain:** Monitor healthcare supply information.
- 🔔 **Alerts & Reports:** Review alerts and dashboard reports.
- 🎨 **Interactive UI:** Modern dashboard interface with demo data.

## 🎯 Objective

The goal of Smart Health is to make healthcare resource management more organized by bringing key operational information into a single dashboard.
